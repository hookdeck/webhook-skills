# Setting Up Simpro Webhooks

## Prerequisites

- A Simpro build where you have administrator privileges (or ask an administrator to do this)
- Your public webhook endpoint URL (use `npx hookdeck-cli listen 3000 simpro --path /webhooks/simpro` for local development)

## Get Your Signing Secret

Set the Secret on the Simpro webhook subscription to a long random value, then paste the same value here.

The Secret is a random string of your choice, not generated by Simpro. Store the same value as `SIMPRO_WEBHOOK_SECRET`.

## Register Your Endpoint

1. In Simpro, click **System → Setup**.
2. Find **API** near the bottom (if it is missing, you are probably not an administrator), hover over it and click **Webhook Subscriptions**.
3. Click **Create Subscription**.
4. Enter a name, and an email address to receive debugging information.
5. Enter the **Secret** (see above).
6. Enter the **Callback URL** of your endpoint.
7. Select the events to subscribe to and save.

Subscriptions can also be created through the API: [Webhook Subscriptions](https://developer.simprogroup.com/apidoc/?page=ef201d96de80d31272bd099dbc695dfb).

## Test Your Endpoint

After saving, click **Test Subscription**. Simpro posts a `test.updated` event with `reference: { "testID": 1 }` to your Callback URL, signed with your Secret.
